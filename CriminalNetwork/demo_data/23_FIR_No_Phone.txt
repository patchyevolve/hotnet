The caller provided the following information:

The caller refused to identify themselves and ended the call abruptly.

Action Taken:
Anonymous complaint noted. Investigation to be conducted with caution. Information to be cross-verified with existing evidence in FIR 123/2024.

Note: The caller's phone number was traced to a prepaid SIM registered under a fake identity at a cyber cafe in Lajpat Nagar. This suggests the caller may have knowledge of the fraud network but wishes to remain anonymous.
