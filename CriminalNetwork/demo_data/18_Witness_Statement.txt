WITNESS STATEMENT
Recorded at: Nehru Nagar Police Station
Date: 22/03/2024
Time: 11:30 hours
Statement recorded by: SI Vikram Rathore

Witness: Rajesh Kumar, Age 42, Flat 5A, Rose Apartments, Lajpat Nagar Part II

Statement:
I, Rajesh Kumar, am a resident of Rose Apartments, Lajpat Nagar Part II, New Delhi. I have been living in this apartment complex for the past 8 years.

I know Meena Devi (Flat 4B) as my neighbor. She is a widow and lives alone with her two children.

On 12th March 2024, at around 9:15 AM, I was leaving for my office when I noticed a man standing near the entrance of our building. He was wearing a blue shirt and talking on his mobile phone. I did not recognize him as a resident or someone I had seen before.

On 13th March 2024, I saw the same person again at approximately 8:45 AM. He was standing near the gate and appeared to be waiting for someone.

On 14th March 2024, at around 9:00 AM, I saw two persons near Meena Devi's flat. One of them was the same man I had seen earlier. The second person was shorter and wearing a white shirt. They were talking to each other and then entered the building.

At around 9:15 AM on the same day, I heard Meena Devi's phone ring. She answered the call and I could hear her speaking in a confused tone. She said things like "kya baat hai" and "maine toh kuch nahi kiya".

Later that evening, Meena Devi came to my flat and told me she had been cheated of Rs. 15 lakhs. She was very distressed. She said someone had called her pretending to be from the bank and she had shared her OTP.

I am willing to testify to these observations in court.

Signature: Rajesh Kumar
Date: 22/03/2024
