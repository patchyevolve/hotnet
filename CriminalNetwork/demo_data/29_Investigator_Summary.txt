INVESTIGATION SUMMARY (DERIVED FROM FIR 123/2024)
Prepared by: SI Vikram Rathore
Date: 20/03/2024

Summary of Investigation:
Based on FIR 123/2024 filed by Meena Devi, the following facts have been established:

1. On 12 March 2024, Meena Devi received a call from an unknown number claiming to be from SBI Customer Care
2. On 13 March 2024, a second call was received and Meena Devi shared her account details and OTP
3. On 14 March 2024, three bank transfers totaling Rs. 15,00,000 were made from her account
4. The transfers were made to accounts ending in 1234, 7891, and 5678

CDR Analysis:
- Phone records show calls from Rakesh Kumar (9876543210) to Meena Devi (9876543213) on 12, 13, and 14 March
- Calls were made from Karol Bagh Tower 1 and Nehru Nagar Tower 2
- Duration of calls ranged from 5 to 10 minutes

Bank Analysis:
- Rs. 5,00,000 transferred to HDFC account ending 1234 (Suresh Kumar)
- Rs. 5,00,000 transferred to PNB account ending 7891 (Suresh Kumar)
- Rs. 5,00,000 transferred to BoB account ending 5678 (Amit Sharma)

CCTV Analysis:
- Male persons observed near Meena Devi's residence on 12, 13, and 14 March
- Witness Rajesh Kumar corroborates CCTV observations

Conclusion:
Based on the evidence from FIR 123/2024, CDR analysis, bank records, and CCTV footage, the fraud appears to have been orchestrated by Rakesh Kumar with assistance from Suresh Kumar and Amit Sharma.

Note: This summary is derived from the original FIR and should not be treated as independent evidence.
