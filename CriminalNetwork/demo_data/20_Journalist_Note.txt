JOURNALIST NOTE - FOR PUBLICATION REVIEW
Source: Priya Singh, Crime Correspondent, Delhi Herald
Date: 28/03/2024
Case Reference: FIR 123/2024

Key Points:
- Three arrested in Rs. 15 lakh bank fraud case
- Suspects used fake bank calls to extract OTP from victim
- Main accused Rakesh Kumar (32) arrested from Karol Bagh
- Associates Suresh Kumar (29) and Amit Sharma (27) also arrested
- Previous criminal records found for all three suspects

Details:
Delhi Police has arrested three persons in connection with a Rs. 15 lakh bank fraud case. The accused allegedly called the victim, Meena Devi (34), impersonating bank officials and obtained her account details and OTP.

The main accused, Rakesh Kumar, runs a mobile phone shop called RK Telecom in Karol Bagh. He has two prior convictions for similar fraud schemes. His associates Suresh Kumar and Amit Sharma handled the financial operations and technical aspects of the fraud.

According to police, the accused transferred Rs. 5 lakh each to three different bank accounts belonging to the suspects. The money was quickly moved through multiple accounts to obscure the trail.

SI Vikram Rathore, the investigating officer, said the arrests were made based on CDR analysis, CCTV footage, and witness statements. "The suspects used multiple phone numbers and created a complex network of transactions to evade detection," he said.

All three suspects have been remanded to judicial custody. The investigation is ongoing to identify any additional accomplices.
